Welcome in Pakistan

A small doorway
to a world of possibility.

A presentation of Pakistan's resources, craftsmanship, enterprise and places of opportunity for buyers and partners around the world.

Though the doorway may seem modest, step inside and discover a country with much to show the world.

Pakistan is home to makers, growers, engineers, artists and exporters whose work reaches well beyond its borders. This introduction brings those stories together, from city workshops to the coast of the Arabian Sea.

A portrait of Pakistan

I am
Pakistan.

I am not only the headlines. I am Abdul Sattar Edhi's philanthropy and Ansar Burney's compassion. I am the courage of Rashid Minhas, the calligraphy of Sadequain, and the brilliance of Arfa Karim. I am the 1992 Cricket World Cup, Sharmeen Obaid's Oscar, and the voices of Noor Jehan and Mehdi Hassan.

I am home to the ancient civilizations of the Indus Valley, Moenjodaro, Taxila and Harappa. The Himalayas and Hindu Kush are part of me; K2 is my strength. I am Shandur, Gwadar, the Karakoram Highway, and the salt of Khewra. I am many communities, cultures and languages, connected by resilience and hospitality.

I am a sea of untapped potential.
Yes, I am Pakistan.

A gateway to regional trade

Gwadar
opens a horizon.

Set on the Arabian Sea, Gwadar is a deep-water port with modern nautical infrastructure and multipurpose vessel facilities. Its location gives Pakistan a compelling platform for regional connectivity and future trade partnerships.

Trade is how
we meet.

“From the Silk Route to today's supply chains, our lives are linked by the exchange of goods, ideas and skills.”

Across centuries, people have traded to share what they make and build new livelihoods. Today, every connection between a producer and a buyer can help businesses grow and communities thrive.
